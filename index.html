How to play

  1. Hold a colored button to move that part. Let go to stop.
  2. The colors match the arm picture: blue turns, green lifts, yellow bends, purple tilts the wrist, teal twists, orange is the hand.
  3. Challenge: turn until the blue line points at the orange dot, reach the ball, Open the hand, then Close it to grab. Each grab earns a star.
  4. Red STOP stops everything.

Right now this is practice: the buttons move the picture only. The real arm stays locked until its setup is finished. For real servo power, the bench supply OUTPUT switch is the true off switch.

On a computer: arrow keys turn and lift, W A S D bend and wrist (hold two keys to move two parts together), 1–4 speed (slow to fast), Space stops. The corner-arrows button opens the full-screen drive view.
